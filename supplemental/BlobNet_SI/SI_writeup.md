# Blob-Net supplemental information

This is the curated supplemental document used with the current manuscript.
It contains the gold-in-TiO2 comparison followed by ten selected supplemental
figures covering synthetic image formation, training histories,
cross-geometry generalization, edge behavior, experimental preprocessing,
scale sensitivity, receptive field, and noise robustness.

The larger numbered experiment suite generated during development is not part
of this tracked document. Its caches, training runs, alternate panels, and PNG
previews remain reproducible working outputs rather than publication sources.

Experimental comparisons show model agreement and differences without
independently known atom coordinates. They must not be interpreted as direct
accuracy measurements.
